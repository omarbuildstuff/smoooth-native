# Smoooth — Design Context

## Users
Developers, educators, and content creators recording tutorials, product demos, and
walkthroughs on macOS. They want a polished, "it just looks pro" result with zero manual
keyframing — the app auto-creates cinematic pan/zoom from cursor activity.

## Brand Personality
Confident · cinematic · effortless. The product's signature is *smooth motion* — the name is
the promise. It should feel like a creative tool a designer would choose, not a utility.

## Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme: dark.** Used during focused recording/editing sessions; dark is the right context
  and matches the original "ocean-blue" identity (deep navy `hsl(220 25% 6%)`, one confident
  blue accent `hsl(213 94% 68%)`). Light mode supported but dark is primary.
- **Native, not web.** SF Pro for UI/body; **SF Pro Rounded** (heavy, large, tight tracking)
  for the wordmark/marketing headings — a distinctive yet native voice (no bundled web fonts).
- **Bold marketing, calm editor.** Home + Recorder are first-impression surfaces: dramatic
  scale, asymmetric layout, a signature on-brand hero (the framed-video "output" look), and one
  orchestrated staggered entrance. The Editor is a work surface: calm, legible, usable —
  hierarchy and rhythm, not drama.
- **Signature moment:** a tilted, shadowed, padded rounded-video frame on a gradient backdrop
  with a cursor + zoom-ring — it literally shows what the app produces.

## Design Principles
1. **The output is the hero.** Show the framed-zoom result, don't describe it.
2. **One confident accent.** Brand blue is rare and owns the primary action; everything else is
   tinted navy neutrals. 60/30/10 by weight.
3. **Dramatic hierarchy on marketing, quiet hierarchy in the editor.** Big wordmark vs small
   supporting text; never a flat scale.
4. **Native motion.** Staggered ease-out entrances + a single live pulse (record), gated by
   Reduce Motion. No bounce, no gratuitous micro-animations.
5. **No AI slop.** No purple→blue gradients, no glassmorphism-everywhere, no gradient text, no
   neon. Distinctive because it's *crafted and on-brand*, not because it has more effects.

(Native macOS / SwiftUI port; the web-specific impeccable tactics — OKLCH CSS, clamp, container
queries, web fonts — are adapted to SwiftUI equivalents.)
